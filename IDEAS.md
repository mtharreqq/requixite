# Ideas

Loose ideas, not commitments. Each one may change, merge or be dropped once it's tried. Add new ones freely; move an idea to the changelog when it ships.

## /about

- **Tell my story.** Who I am, my experience, what I've been through.
- **Pick how to read it.** Full story, a summary, or a bullet list.
- **Inline logos.** When the text names a company, app or tool, show its logo in the sentence. Pattern: RantAI landing `home-solution.tsx`. Logos already in `public/logos`, `public/logomark`, `public/ai-logos`.
- **Listen instead of read.** Text to speech for the about story, for people who'd rather not read. Could later carry over to the use cases if they become blog-style posts.
- **Pictures.** Images related to the story. What they are is still open.

## Open questions

- Do the three reading modes share one source text, or are they written separately?
- Text to speech: browser `speechSynthesis` (free, voice varies by device) or pre-recorded/generated audio (same voice everywhere, a file per language)?
